Prometheus stole fire from the gods. His punishment was an eagle eating his liver every single day — for eternity.

In Greek mythology, Zeus had withheld fire from humans, keeping them weak and animalistic. Prometheus, a Titan, sided with mortals over the gods.

He hid burning embers inside a hollow fennel stalk and smuggled them down from Mount Olympus. Humanity could now cook food, forge metal, and survive winter. Civilization was born.

Zeus chained Prometheus to a rock in the Caucasus mountains. Every single day, an eagle ate his liver, which regenerated overnight. The torment was eternal — until Hercules eventually freed him centuries later.
