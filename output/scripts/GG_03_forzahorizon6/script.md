# GG_03_forzahorizon6 — Forza Horizon 6

**Voice:** Adam  
**Word count:** 138  

## Hook
Racing franchises have gone to Mexico, Australia, France, and the UK. Forza Horizon 6 goes to Japan — and it's the biggest map the series has ever built.

## Gameplay
550 licensed cars, from a 1967 Toyota 2000GT to a 2026 Nissan GT-R concept. Ranked races earn you a standing in the Horizon leaderboard — and the top performers unlock a gold wristband that opens Legend Island, an exclusive section of the map no casual player ever sees.

## Standout
Playground Games rebuilt the open world from scratch for Japan's geography — mountain passes, neon city circuits, coastal routes, and off-road terrain that shifts every season. It's not just the biggest Forza. It's the most complete one.

## CTA
Releases May 19 on PC and Xbox Series X|S. Premium Edition owners are already in — wishlist now on Steam.
