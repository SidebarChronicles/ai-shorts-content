## How to read this report

- **Avg view %** is the most predictive metric for shorts. Above 70% = excellent, below 30% = hook problem.
- **The retention curve** shows where viewers dropped off. A sharp dip in the first 10% means the hook didn't land. A flat curve means people watched to the end.
- **Subscriber conversion** of 0.5%+ is strong for a faceless / fact-based channel.
- **Engagement rate** of 4%+ indicates the content provokes reaction.
