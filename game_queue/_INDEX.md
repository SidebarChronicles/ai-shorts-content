## How to add a game

Create `game_queue/GG_NN_<slug>.md` following the template in `skill/game-short/SKILL.md`.
Good candidates:
- Upcoming releases with strong trailers and distinct hooks
- New releases in the last 30 days that haven't been covered by faceless channels yet
- Games with surprising mechanics that can be explained in 4 beats

## Steam sourcing

Steam's "Coming Soon" page (store.steampowered.com/explore/upcoming) is the best
funnel for upcoming game shorts. Filter by interest and score candidates on:
- Visual quality of trailer (will it read well in portrait crop?)
- Hook strength (is there one surprising mechanic or fact?)
- Audience size potential (wishlists, press coverage)
