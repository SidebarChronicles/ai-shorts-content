# SY_05_H_basement — The basement door doesn't lock from this side

**Status:** QUEUED
**Subgenre:** horror
**Voice:** Daniel
**Model:** eleven_multilingual_v2

## Premise
Narrator just moved into a fixer-upper. Discovers the basement door has a deadbolt — but it only locks from the outside. There's no handle on the inside. Narrator initially writes it off as old construction. Then they notice the scratches.

## Hook angle
"The basement door has a lock. The lock is on the wrong side."

## Visual style notes
- Palette: near-monochrome, blue-black shadows, single warm light source (a bare bulb)
- Avoid: showing what's behind the door clearly. Hint only.

## Notes
- Original micro-fiction. No SCP/Backrooms/etc. tropes verbatim.
- Mid_anchor: in the basement, narrator finds rows of identical objects neatly arranged — and one of them is their own.
- CTA: "Don't open it."
