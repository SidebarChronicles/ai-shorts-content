# SY_06_H_lighthouse — The lighthouse keeper's logs are written in my handwriting

**Status:** QUEUED
**Subgenre:** horror
**Voice:** Daniel
**Model:** eleven_multilingual_v2

## Premise
Narrator is a new lighthouse keeper at a remote station. Day-one task: review the prior keeper's logs. They open the logbook. The handwriting is identical to theirs. The most recent entry is dated tomorrow.

## Hook angle
"I opened the logbook. The handwriting was mine. The date was tomorrow."

## Visual style notes
- Palette: stormy sea blues, single warm lamp light over a desk, salt-water blacks
- Avoid: showing narrator's face — first-person POV throughout

## Notes
- Original micro-fiction. The "you are the haunting" trope, executed concisely.
- Mid_anchor: the dated-tomorrow entry describes, in detail, the narrator dropping the logbook in the next 30 seconds — and the narrator drops it
- CTA: "Don't read ahead."
