# 10 — The Billionaire's Pilot Who Traded On The Boss's Tips

**Status:** QUEUED
**Narrative score:** 9/10 — character-driven, quotable evidence
**Source:** [DOJ press release, February 26, 2024](https://www.justice.gov/usao-sdny/pr/personal-pilot-billionaire-investor-joseph-lewis-pleads-guilty-insider-trading-scheme)
**Defendant:** Patrick O'Connor, 67, Preston Hollow NY (personal pilot)
**Co-defendant:** Joseph Lewis, 87, British billionaire (already pled guilty Jan 24, 2024)
**Court:** U.S. District Court, Southern District of New York (Judge Jessica G.L. Clarke)
**Sentence:** Pled guilty Feb 26, 2024 — faces 25 yrs maximum
**Conviction basis:** Guilty plea — securities fraud + conspiracy

## Hook

The boss was a billionaire. The pilot flew his jet. When the boss said "buy" — the pilot bought. The boss even wired him $500K to do it.

## Arc

Patrick O'Connor, 67, was the personal pilot to Joe Lewis — one of Britain's richest men, principal owner of Tavistock Group. Through his investments, Lewis controlled board seats at multiple companies and got material non-public information. Lewis tipped his pilot. On one occasion, after learning about a coming announcement at biotech Mirati Therapeutics, Lewis told O'Connor to buy the stock. He wired $500,000 to O'Connor to fund the purchases. O'Connor wrote in encrypted WhatsApp messages: "talked with Mr. Lewis," "we will make much more within the next 6 weeks," "All conversations on app is encrypted so all good." He added the "Boss has inside info" and "knows the outcome." When the announcement came, the stock spiked. O'Connor pleaded guilty in February 2024. Lewis had pled guilty a month earlier.

## Key quotes

> "PATRICK O'CONNOR worked as a personal pilot to his co-defendant in this case, JOSEPH LEWIS, for several years. LEWIS is a billionaire businessman and investor who is the principal owner of the Tavistock Group, an international private investment organization."

> "On one occasion, after receiving inside information from LEWIS concerning Mirati Therapeutics, O'CONNOR wrote in a WhatsApp message to a friend that he had 'talked with Mr. Lewis,' 'we will make much more within the next 6 weeks with Mirati,' and 'think we have people who know.'"

> "Within days, LEWIS wired $500,000 to O'CONNOR, and O'CONNOR then used the $500,000 to purchase Mirati stock."

> "On or about October 22, 2019, O'CONNOR texted his same friend that October 28, 2019, was the 'big day for MRTX.' O'CONNOR then added that he thought 'the Boss has inside info' and 'knows the outcome' because 'otherwise why would he make us invest.'"
