**Status:** QUEUED

## Hook

He called them "the Team." Egyptian government officials he paid $4.8 million in bribes to win nearly $140 million in coal contracts. He even took a $200K kickback for himself.

## Arc

Charles Hunter Hobson, 50, was a VP at Corsa Coal Corporation responsible for international sales. Between 2016 and 2020, he conspired to bribe Egyptian government officials — referring to them in writing as "the Team" — to win coal contracts with Al Nasr Company for Coke and Chemicals, then a state-owned Egyptian chemical manufacturer. Bribes were paid through an Egyptian intermediary who received $4.8 million in purported "sales commissions." The intermediary kicked back $200,000+ to Hobson personally. The bribes won Corsa nearly $140 million in coal supply contracts. Federal jury convicted on FCPA + money laundering + wire fraud charges. Co-defendant Cushmore previously pleaded guilty.

> "Between 2016 and 2020, Hobson, who was then a vice president at Corsa responsible for international sales, conspired to and did pay bribes to Egyptian government officials — whom Hobson referred to as 'the Team' — in order to win contracts for Corsa to sell coal products to Al Nasr."
