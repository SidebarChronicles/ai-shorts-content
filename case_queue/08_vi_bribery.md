# 08 — The Public Commissioner Who Rigged a $1.43M Contract

**Status:** QUEUED
**Narrative score:** 8/10
**Source:** [DOJ press release, January 23, 2026](https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/former-commissioner-virgin-islands-department-sports-parks-and-recreation-sentenced-bribery)
**Defendant:** Calvert White, former Commissioner of U.S. Virgin Islands Dept. of Sports, Parks & Recreation
**Co-defendant:** Benjamin Hendricks (intermediary), 68 months
**Court:** U.S. District Court, District of the Virgin Islands
**Sentence:** 5 years prison
**Conviction basis:** Jury convicted of honest services wire fraud + bribery concerning federally funded programs

## Hook

He took a bribe to rig a public contract. The FBI was already watching.

## Arc

Calvert White, former Commissioner of the U.S. Virgin Islands Department of Sports, Parks and Recreation, demanded a bribe in December 2023 from contractor David Whitaker — in exchange for steering a $1.43 million government contract to install security cameras at SP&R properties. White used co-conspirator Benjamin Hendricks as an intermediary. White provided Whitaker with confidential bid information and took official action to encourage awarding the contract. Whitaker sent the bribe to Hendricks's bank account; Hendricks delivered the cash to White. The FBI approached the defendants in June 2024 — seven months in. Jury convicted both. White: 5 years. Hendricks: 68 months.
