# 07 — The Board Director Who Tipped Off His Friends

**Status:** QUEUED
**Narrative score:** 8/10
**Source:** [DOJ press release, Dec 17, 2025](https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/four-individuals-convicted-insider-trading-scheme)
**Defendant:** Rouzbeh "Ross" Haghighat, 61, West Newbury MA + 3 co-defendants
**Court:** U.S. District Court, District of New Jersey (Newark federal jury)
**Sentence:** Convicted at trial Dec 17, 2025 — sentencing scheduled May 4, 2026 (Haghighat faces max 380 yrs)
**Conviction basis:** Jury verdict — securities fraud + 16 counts insider trading + 2 conspiracy

## Hook

He sat on the board. He knew the $3.2 billion merger was coming. So he tipped his friends — and they bought the stock.

## Arc

Ross Haghighat, 61, was a director at a Seattle biopharmaceutical company. In May 2023, he learned a competitor was preparing a confidential $3.2 billion acquisition offer at a premium share price. Instead of waiting for the public announcement, he bought the stock himself — and texted three friends (Kirstyn Pearl, Fabio Sabzevari, James Roberge) the tip. They all loaded up. When the merger was announced in June 2023, the share price spiked. Profit: $600,000+ across the four. Postal inspectors traced it. A Newark jury convicted all four in Dec 2025. Sentencing May 2026.

## Key quotes

> "A federal jury in Newark, New Jersey convicted four individuals today for their participation in a scheme to trade securities on the basis of material nonpublic information about the $3.2 billion merger of two companies, which resulted in illicit profits of over $600,000."

> "In his position as a director in May 2023, Haghighat obtained material nonpublic inside information about another pharmaceutical company's proposed acquisition of Company-1, including sensitive deal terms. He then purchased securities, and tipped others — including Pearl, Sabzevari, and Roberge — for personal benefit with the expectation that they would purchase securities of Company-1."

> "Collectively, the defendants profited more than $600,000 from their purchases of Company-1 securities based on material nonpublic information."

> "Haghighat faces a maximum penalty of 380 years in prison. Pearl faces a maximum penalty of 60 years in prison. Sabzavari faces a maximum penalty of 160 years in prison. Roberge faces a maximum penalty of 160 years in prison."
