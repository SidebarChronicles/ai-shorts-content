## Hook
A man in a suit hijacked a passenger jet, collected $200,000 in cash, and jumped out the back with a parachute. He's never been identified.

## 4-beat arc
- **What is it:** The D.B. Cooper hijacking — the only unsolved skyjacking in U.S. history.
- **Setup:** November 24, 1971. A man calling himself Dan Cooper bought a one-way ticket from Portland to Seattle. He told a flight attendant he had a bomb. Demanded $200,000 in cash and 4 parachutes.
- **Standout:** After the plane refueled in Seattle and released the passengers, Cooper had the pilots fly south at low altitude with the rear staircase lowered. Somewhere over southwest Washington, he jumped. The FBI never found him.
- **CTA:** 45 years of investigation. 800 suspects. $5,800 in decaying bills recovered along the Columbia River in 1980. The FBI officially closed the case in 2016 — Cooper's identity still unknown.
